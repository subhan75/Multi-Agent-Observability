# Returns
Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in original packaging.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of receiving the return.

# Shipping
Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days.
Orders with status "backordered" ship as soon as stock arrives, usually within 14 days.

# Cancellations
Orders can be cancelled while status is "placed" or "backordered". Shipped orders must be returned instead.
